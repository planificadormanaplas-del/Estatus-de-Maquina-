## Phrases that LOOK like AI tells but ARE in your voice

Some warm phrases (e.g. "Thanks for reaching out!", "Great meeting you both!", "happy to answer any additional questions you have") might appear on naive AI-tell lists, but if YOU actually use them and they sound right in your voice, the skill should preserve them — not strip them.

The reason these aren't tells: AI generates them as part of a *cluster* of formulaic phrases. A real person uses them singly, embedded in otherwise specific and direct prose. The presence of one of these is fine. The presence of three of them stacked together (e.g., "Thanks for reaching out! I hope this finds you well. I'd be more than happy to assist with your inquiry.") is the AI tell.
